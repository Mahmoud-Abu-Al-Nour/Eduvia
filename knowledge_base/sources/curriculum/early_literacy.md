# Curriculum Pedagogical Guide: Early Literacy

## Purpose
Provide grounding principles and developmental progressions for foundational literacy instruction in Eduvia MVP.

## Scope and Units
1. **Letter Recognition**: Uppercase letters, lowercase letters, uppercase-lowercase matching, target letter discrimination among visual distractors.
2. **Letter Sounds (Phonemic Awareness)**: Initial phoneme identification, matching letters to initial sounds, distinguishing distinct initial phonemes.
3. **Word Recognition**: High-frequency familiar words, matching identical words, pairing words to pictures, word discrimination.
4. **Simple Sequencing & Comprehension**: 2-step and 3-step story events, first/next temporal relationships, sentence-to-picture matching.

## Developmental Progression
Visual Letter Shape Discrimination → Letter-Sound Association → Sight Word Recognition → Simple Sequential Comprehension.
Ensure vocabulary utilizes concrete, familiar objects (e.g., Cat, Sun, Bus, Apple) rather than abstract terminology.

## Activity Modality Recommendations
- Letter Recognition: Multiple choice or visual identification (finding letter in a group).
- Letter Matching: Matching modality (pairing uppercase 'A' to lowercase 'a').
- Word Recognition: Drag and drop (dragging word labels to corresponding pictures).
- Story Comprehension: Ordering modality (arranging 3 chronological pictures of an event).

## Common Misconceptions and Scaffolding
- Confusing mirror letters (b/d, p/q). Provide explicit directional visual cues or anchor icons.
- Ensure audio/screen-reader pronunciation matches standard phonetic sounds.
