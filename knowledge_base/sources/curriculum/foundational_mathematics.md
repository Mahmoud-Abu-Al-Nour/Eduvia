# Curriculum Pedagogical Guide: Foundational Mathematics

## Purpose
Provide grounding principles and developmental progressions for foundational math instruction in Eduvia MVP.

## Scope and Units
1. **Number Sense & Counting (0–10)**: Numeral recognition, 1-to-1 correspondence, counting objects, cardinality, quantity comparison.
2. **Number Sequence**: Before/after relationships, ordering smallest to largest, sequence completion, numerical patterns.
3. **Addition Within 10**: Combining groups, visual representation, adding 1 or 2, simple equations.
4. **Subtraction Within 10**: Taking away, visual representation, remaining counts, subtracting 1 or 2.
5. **Basic Shapes & Classification**: Circles, squares, triangles, rectangles, sorting and matching by visual attributes.

## Developmental Progression
Concrete (physical objects, visual groups) → Semi-Concrete (tallies, dots, icons) → Abstract (numerals and operational symbols +, -).
Learners must demonstrate 1-to-1 counting correspondence before being introduced to formal operational signs.

## Activity Modality Recommendations
- Counting 0–5 & 6–10: Multiple choice with visual object groups, visual identification ("Find the group with 4"), or matching numeral to group.
- Number Sequencing: Ordering modality (scrambled cards to be arranged in ascending order).
- Addition/Subtraction: Drag and drop (combine groups into a basket) or visual identification.
- Shapes: Matching identical shapes or drag and drop sorting into shape bins.

## Common Misconceptions and Scaffolding
- Counting errors usually stem from touching objects faster than saying numerals. Scaffold by highlighting items sequentially.
- Distinguish the symbol '0' as representing empty or none.
