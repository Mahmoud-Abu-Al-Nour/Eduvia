# Visual Accessibility and Contrast Standards

## Purpose
Ensure all instructional content, buttons, visual targets, and text are easily perceivable by learners with low vision, color vision deficiencies, or ocular motor challenges.

## When to Use
Apply across all activity components, theme palettes, typography scales, and visual identification scenes.

## Core Principles
1. **WCAG AAA Contrast**: Aim for a contrast ratio of at least 7:1 for normal text and 4.5:1 for large text / graphical controls.
2. **Color Independence**: Never convey meaning, state (correct/incorrect), or category through color alone; always pair color with text, shape, or icons.
3. **Large Scalable Typography**: Base font size of at least 18px–20px for primary body and instructions, scaling responsively.
4. **Distinct Focus Indicators**: Prominent 3px solid focus ring with high contrast against both the element and page background.

## Practical Guidance
- Interactive Targets: Minimum tap/click target size of 48px by 48px, with at least 12px margin between adjacent targets.
- Icons: Provide unambiguous SVG icons paired with readable labels or accessible text descriptions.

## Do and Do Not
- **Do**: Provide high-contrast outlines around visual objects in counting and identification tasks.
- **Do**: Test palettes against deuteranopia, protanopia, and tritanopia simulators.
- **Do Not**: Place text directly over busy or textured background graphics.
- **Do Not**: Use light pastel text on white backgrounds.

## References and Pedagogical Sources
- W3C Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA / AAA.
- American Foundation for the Blind (AFB). Guidelines for Accessible Design.
