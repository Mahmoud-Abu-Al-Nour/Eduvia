# Spaced Practice and Retrieval Interleaving

## Purpose
Enhance long-term skill retention, generalizability, and fluency by scheduling practice intervals and interleaving related target skills.

## When to Use
Use during retention phases, maintenance checks, and mastery review across learning objectives.

## Core Principles
1. **Distributed Learning**: Multiple short learning sessions produce superior retention compared to massed cramming.
2. **Expanding Intervals**: Gradually increase the time elapsed between reviews of mastered concepts (1 day, 3 days, 1 week).
3. **Interleaving**: Mix current target objectives with previously mastered review items to prevent mechanical answering.
4. **Active Retrieval**: Require active selection, matching, or ordering rather than passive recognition.

## Practical Guidance
- Integrate 1 review question from a prior mastered objective for every 3 new activity items.
- Maintain consistent interaction patterns so the cognitive demand remains on educational retrieval rather than UI comprehension.

## Do and Do Not
- **Do**: Re-verify foundational skills (e.g. counting 0-5) when learner enters addition.
- **Do**: Keep sessions short and calm (5-10 minutes per active learning block).
- **Do Not**: Overload sessions with too many novel concepts simultaneously.
- **Do Not**: Present identical items repeatedly in a single massed block.
