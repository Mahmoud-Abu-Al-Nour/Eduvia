# Problem Statement

Special education systems worldwide face acute systemic bottlenecks:

1. **The Modification Trap**:
   When neurodivergent learners or students with cognitive processing differences struggle with standard instructional materials, the default institutional response is often to simplify the curriculum itself. This creates compounding achievement deficits over time.

2. **Extreme Teacher Burden**:
   Crafting individualized education plans (IEPs) and bespoke multi-sensory activities for classrooms with diverse neurotypes requires hours of manual preparation per lesson. Special educators experience high rates of burnout.

3. **Rigid, Flawed Categorization**:
   Traditional educational software pigeonholes learners into rigid, unscientific buckets (e.g., "Visual Learner" vs "Auditory Learner"). In reality, learning affinity is dynamic, context-dependent, and shifts across different subjects, times of day, and emotional states.

4. **Inaccessible, Over-stimulating Software**:
   Educational tools for children frequently feature aggressive animations, loud audio cues, cluttered visual fields, and high cognitive overhead that overwhelm sensory-sensitive learners.

Eduvia directly resolves these challenges through systematic, automated adaptation of delivery paired with uncompromised curriculum fidelity.
