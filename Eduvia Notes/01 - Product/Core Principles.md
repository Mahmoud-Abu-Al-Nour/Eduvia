# Core Product Principles

All engineering and product design in Eduvia must strictly adhere to these tenets:

---

### Principle 1: Same Curriculum, Personalized Delivery
The learning objective is constant; the path to mastery is variable. If a student struggles to recognize numbers 1 to 5, we do not abandon the objective; we alter the modality (visual dot patterns vs interactive drag-and-drop) and increase scaffolding.

### Principle 2: Empirical Scoring over Rigid Labels
We **never** label a learner as a "Visual Learner" or "ADHD student". The system tracks dynamic efficacy matrices:
$$	ext{Effectiveness} = f(	ext{Score}, 	ext{Confidence}, 	ext{Evidence Count})$$
Affinity is continuous, multifaceted, and evolves with evidence.

### Principle 3: AI as Generator, Not Diagnostician
- Eduvia is **NOT** a clinical diagnostic tool.
- Eduvia does **NOT** make medical classifications.
- Gemini is used to generate structured pedagogical content within verified constraints, not to make autonomous clinical decisions.

### Principle 4: Cognitive Calm & Universal Design
Interfaces must minimize extraneous cognitive load. We eliminate countdown timers that trigger anxiety, avoid garish visual clutter, provide generous click targets, and ensure full screen-reader and keyboard accessibility.
