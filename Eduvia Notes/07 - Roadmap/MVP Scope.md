# MVP Scope

The pilot release scope required for classroom demonstration:

---

1. **Administration & Teacher Management**:
   - Secure teacher login and profile inspection.
   - Demonstration curriculum browsing and inspection.

2. **Standardized Curriculum**:
   - Foundational numeracy curriculum with multi-tiered hierarchy.
   - Clear learning objectives, difficulty levels, and prerequisites.

3. **Learner Interaction (Phase 3–5)**:
   - Simple teacher-assigned learner session.
   - Interactive matching and multiple-choice activity execution.
   - Text-to-speech audio prompt reading.

4. **Telemetry & Calibration (Phase 6–8)**:
   - Immediate accuracy and assistance tracking.
   - Dynamic modality profile updates visible to teacher.
